# crimedatabase
LA Crime Data: Link for Tableau: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/varun4yadav

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of crime trends in Los Angeles between 2010 and 2023. It is designed to assist law enforcement agencies in monitoring crime patterns, optimizing resource allocation, and identifying high-crime areas.

1) Yearly and Monthly Trends: Track long-term and seasonal crime fluctuations to help spot emerging patterns or recurring issues.
2) Most Common Time: Discover the time of day when different types of crimes are more prevalent, allowing for targeted patrol scheduling.
3) Top 5 Areas: Quickly identify the neighborhoods with the highest crime rates, enabling focused interventions and resource deployment.
4) Top Crimes: A breakdown of the most frequent crime categories, helping law enforcement prioritize based on crime severity and volume.
5) Filters (on the right): Customize your view by selecting crime types, date ranges, or specific areas for detailed analysis.
